# ADR-2000: Create Platform Chief

**Status**: Accepted
**Deciders**: Chief AI Platform Architect
**Date**: 2026-07-23
**Tags**: organization, chief, v3.0, enterprise

## Context
The Cosca framework needed to expand its organizational coverage to include enterprise domains that were previously uncovered or inadequately covered by existing chiefs.

## Decision
Create the Platform Chief role as a first-class department in the Cosca organizational structure, with dedicated SKILL.md, specialists, and council representation.

## Rationale
Internal Developer Platform (IDP) is essential for developer productivity at scale. Previously fragmented across DevOps and Automation Chiefs. A dedicated Platform Chief owns golden paths, developer portal, and self-service tooling to reduce cognitive load on feature teams.

## Alternatives Considered
1. Extend DevOps Chief scope: DevOps focuses on CI/CD, not developer experience. 2. Extend Automation Chief scope: Automation focuses on scripts, not platform. 3. Dedicated Platform Chief: Correct scope for IDP and DX.

## Decision Outcome
Create dedicated Platform Chief with:
- Reports to: as defined in ORGCHART.md
- Specialists: as defined in departments/platform/SKILL.md
- Council representation: as defined in COUNCILS.md

## Consequences
- Clear ownership for the domain
- Dedicated focus and accountability
- Better cross-department coordination through council representation
- Increased framework size (14 new departments)

## Compliance
- All new departments follow CONVENTIONS.md format
- All departments have AGENT_DNA.md-compliant contracts
- All departments have council representation
