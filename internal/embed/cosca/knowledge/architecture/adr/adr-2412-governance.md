# ADR-2412: Create Governance Chief

**Status**: Accepted
**Deciders**: Chief AI Platform Architect
**Date**: 2026-07-23
**Tags**: organization, chief, v3.0, enterprise

## Context
The Cosca framework needed to expand its organizational coverage to include enterprise domains that were previously uncovered or inadequately covered by existing chiefs.

## Decision
Create the Governance Chief role as a first-class department in the Cosca organizational structure, with dedicated SKILL.md, specialists, and council representation.

## Rationale
Framework governance ensures quality, consistency, and evolution of the Cosca itself. Previously implicit in Kernel and CTO responsibilities. A dedicated Governance Chief enforces conventions, detects duplication, and manages RFC process.

## Alternatives Considered
1. Keep within Kernel: Kernel should be runtime, not governance. 2. Keep within CEO/CTO: Too busy with strategic decisions. 3. Dedicated Governance Chief: Correct separation of concerns.

## Decision Outcome
Create dedicated Governance Chief with:
- Reports to: as defined in ORGCHART.md
- Specialists: as defined in departments/governance/SKILL.md
- Council representation: as defined in COUNCILS.md

## Consequences
- Clear ownership for the domain
- Dedicated focus and accountability
- Better cross-department coordination through council representation
- Increased framework size (14 new departments)

## Compliance
- All new departments follow CONVENTIONS.md format
- All departments have AGENT_DNA.md-compliant contracts
- All departments have council representation
